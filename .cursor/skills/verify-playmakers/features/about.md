# About page

## Sub-features

- Product explainer without replay controls.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Click **About** in the top bar.

## Driving it with Playwright

Click link `About`. Assert that `Replay controls` and the `Pause` button are gone, and that heading `One match. A whole production crew.` is visible. The card must be centred and at most 1392 px wide.
